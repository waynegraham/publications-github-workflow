<p>The backdrop for this study is that the Digital Library Federation (DLF) has become increasingly concerned by the absence of reliable information with which to document and explain changing patterns of library use in universities and colleges. DLF repeatedly hears from academic library directors that such analyses are vital but missing ingredients of their strategic planning, and of the business case they make to faculty and senior administrators either to win or bolster support for the library and its changing directions.</p>

<p>This four-part series highlights the careers and achievements of former CLIR data and software curation fellows. In 2022, Inna Kouper&mdash;a 2012-2014 CLIR data curation fellow herself&mdash;interviewed eleven former data and software curation fellows who received fellowships between the years 2015 and 2018. The work of these and 40 more fellows was made possible with the generous support of the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation. Dr. Kouper talked to each about their fellowship experiences, their careers, and the challenges of data and software curation, preparing profiles that capture the breadth and diversity of their work while summarizing their perspectives on current practice.</p>

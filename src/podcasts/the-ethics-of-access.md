<p>How can recordings of indigenous languages be made accessible to the communities they represent? In this episode of <em>Material Memory</em>, we talk to experts about the ethical considerations and complexities of providing broad access to recordings that may be culturally sensitive—sacred sounds, songs and language—and why it’s important to reconnect people to their own content. One lesson? The story doesn’t end once something is digitized.</p>

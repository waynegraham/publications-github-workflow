<p>&quot;The Chinese Archive: A Pocket Manual&quot; by co-authors, Yasser Ali Nasser and Matthew Wong Foreman is a pioneering guide that illuminates the current archival crisis in the People&rsquo;s Republic of China (PRC) while offering invaluable, pragmatic advice to budding scholars navigating a tumultuous era of global academic transformation. &quot;The Chinese Archive: A Pocket Manual&quot; is part of the CLIR Pocket Burgundy series which showcases succinct publications addressing a spectrum of subjects within the information and cultural heritage community.</p>

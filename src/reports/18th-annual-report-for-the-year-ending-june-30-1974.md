<p>The 1973-74 program of the Council on Library Resources, a private operating foundation devoted to the solution of library problems, is reported. The council&#39;s board of directors, committees, officers, and staff are listed. Summaries are given of the council&#39;s activities in the following areas: (1) libraries and the developing technology, (2) national library services, (3) automation and networks, (4) academic and research libraries, (5) the public library, (6) archives and special collections, (7) preservation and conservation, (8) microforms, reprography, and nonprint media, (9) international cooperation, (10) professional development, and (11) grants and contracts.</p>
